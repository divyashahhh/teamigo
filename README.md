**1.3 Motivation**
Our project aims to serve two different stakeholders – the organisation and the individual. Divya and I, Atharva, have real-life experiences as each of these stakeholders that motivate us to pursue this project.

Atharva: As the co-founder of Blitzcric, a youth-led Cricket Club Business, I have firsthand experience with the organisational challenges that come with entrepreneurship in the sports industry—from customer management and membership subscriptions to community building, event planning, merchandise sales, and communication. To manage these essential functions, I often find myself using multiple tools, such as WhatsApp, PayLah, Google Sheets, and others. Existing platforms that attempt to integrate these functions into a single solution are either too expensive or overly complex, making them impractical for early-stage businesses like mine. Through this experience, I realised that these core organisational needs are not unique to my club but can be abstracted across various businesses, clubs, and communities. Moreover, many of these organisations would benefit from a standardised yet flexible solution—one that offers a minimalist, intuitive UI while still providing sufficient customisable options to suit niche needs. This insight is what drives me to pursue this project!

Divya: As a passionate sports enthusiast, I’ve always believed that sports are more than just games—they’re about community, connection, and shared passion. Growing up, I struggled to find people to spontaneously play with, especially outside of school or structured teams. Whether it was a casual soccer match at the park or a weekend cricket game, I noticed how difficult it was to bring together like-minded friends. Too often, I found myself excited to play but left without enough players to start a proper game. I knew I wasn’t alone; many others faced the same challenge of wanting to stay active, improve their skills, or simply have fun through sports but lacked an easy way to find teammates, set up matches, or discover new playing opportunities. That’s where I found my motivation for Teamigo—a platform built for sports enthusiasts of all levels who are looking to connect with others nearby, join pick-up games, or even start their casual teams/organisations. Whether you’re a competitive player or just someone looking for a fun way to stay fit, Teamigo makes it easy to match with others who share your interests, schedule games, and become part of an active sports community in Singapore. No more struggling to find partners for a tennis match or waiting around at the court hoping others show up!

**1.4 Aim**
As mentioned earlier, two stakeholders benefit from Teamigo. Hence, our project has the following two aims:

Organisation – Teamigo gives access to an affordable, easy-to-use platform that empowers new community leaders to efficiently manage their operations and members without the burden of multiple disjointed tools.

Individual – Teamigo aims to provide a platform where one can effortlessly find communities, clubs or other such organisations that specialise in activities of the individual’s interest. As such, it makes it easier for one to connect with others who share similar interests. 

**1.5 User Stories**

Organisation

As an organisation that needs an online ecosystem dedicated to its activities and members/ customers which does not have the resources (time/ money/ experience/ technical knowledge) to build its app (each registered host gets their private ecosystem that we will call a ‘Portal’), they will register as a host and obtain a Teamigo portal.
As the head of a community who wants to keep their members updated about its affairs, I want to be able to make posts about events/ promotions/ announcements, post polls to collect availability/ interest for my events, and post advertisements on the “Announcements Page” which I can do on Teamigo’s announcements page.
As a business that wants to sell their products and merchandise that have been advertised on the “Announcements Page” to the customers who have subscribed to/ joined its Teamigo portal, they can post the product details on the “Merchandise Page”.
As an organisation’s administrator that needs to keep track of its members’ details (personal info, transactions), they can access an “Analytics Page” that shows the individual profiles and relevant transactions to their Teamigo portal.
A sports club may have multiple teams, i.e. boys, girls, age groups or teams for different tournaments that need to be managed separately. The teams can be separated into multiple chats on the “Chats Page” while still being part of the same portal.
As a growing organisation with an increasing number of events and activities, these can be neatly organised by the host, which will then be updated on each member's “Calendar Page”.
As a growing organisation hosting an event that needs more traction, I can post a public invitation on the “Match-Up Page” to reach out to interested individuals. Alternatively, while hosting a private event exclusive to organisation members, I can post private invites for members to accept via the same page.

Individual

As a user who wants to find some activities to do, they can hop onto Teamigo and find clubs and communities near their area that interest them, via the Map extension in the “Match-Up Page”. The user can also browse through public event invites and join events of interest via the same page.
As a portal member who is looking for others to engage with, they can browse through the private event invites and join events of interest. The member can also access the “Calendar Page” to view all events hosted by their subscribed Portals. Furthermore, they can chat and network with other members via the “Chats Page”. Lastly, members can purchase their portals’ merchandise/ products via the “Merchandise Page”.






**1.6 Scope of Project**

Teamigo is a vibrant, community-driven platform that offers a private digital ecosystem where organisations of all sizes can seamlessly connect with their members while empowering individuals to discover and engage with communities that inspire them.
1.7 Features 
Feature 1 (Core): Signup & Login Page 
Description: Secure login with role-based access and profile management
Feature 2 (Core): User Onboarding 
Description: A guided onboarding process where new users set up their profiles by entering personal details, interests, and uploading a profile picture
Feature 3 (Core): Announcements Page
Description: A central hub for organisations to post news, updates, and notifications
Feature 4 (Core): Merchandise Page
Description: A storefront-style interface where users can browse club merchandise, view product details, and, later, complete purchases via online payments
Feature 5 (Core): “Match-Up” Page
Description: Using a shared forum page where users can upload requests for members, a feature to connect users based on common interests or complementary skills within their organisation
Feature 6 (Core): Chats Page
Description: Real-time messaging for one-on-one or group conversations, ensuring seamless communication among members
Feature 7 (Core): Calendar Page
Description: A shared calendar that displays events, meetings, and club activities, with support for reminders and event management
Extension 1: Location/Map-based Discovery (embedded within Match-Up)
Description: Integration with mapping APIs (like Google Maps or Mapbox) to help users discover nearby events and connect with others based on geographic proximity
Extension 2: Analytics for Hosts
Description: A dashboard that provides hosts with detailed analytics on user engagement, event participation, and overall community activity. This will help hosts monitor and improve their organisational outreach
Extension 3: Feedback and Q&A
Description: A feature enabling users to submit feedback, ask questions, and view answers. Moderators or hosts can manage these inputs to foster community improvement and engagement
Extension 4: Online Payment Methods for Merchandise Page
Description: Integration with payment gateways (e.g. Apple/Google Pay, PayLah!/Paynow) to facilitate secure online transactions, making it easier for users to purchase merchandise directly from the app
